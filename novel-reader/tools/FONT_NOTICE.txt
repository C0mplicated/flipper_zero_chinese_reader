font12.bin is a derived fixed-record serialization of the BDF glyphs.
Pixel positions are mapped to 12x12 cells without resampling, with baseline 10.
Small glyphs extending past that cell are shifted into it without scaling.
ASCII uses the firmware font; non-BMP and oversized glyphs are omitted.
The complete source BDF and generation script accompany the binary so it can
be modified and regenerated. The font remains under its original license.
